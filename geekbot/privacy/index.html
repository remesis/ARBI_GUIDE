Geekbot Privacy Policy

Effective August 1, 2026

This policy describes how Geekbot, Discord application ID 1127782614045048942, processes information while providing community features in private Discord servers managed by the Arbitration Goons community.

Information Geekbot processes

  • Discord account and server data: user, role, channel, message, attachment, interaction, and server identifiers and metadata supplied by Discord when needed to run configured features.
  • Message content and attachments: processed when needed for keyword replies, channel-rule enforcement, message counting, duplicate checks, meme generation, and other commands. This content is normally processed transiently and is not stored as a permanent message archive by Geekbot.
  • Newcomer progress: Discord user IDs, message counts, and update timestamps are stored locally to manage onboarding. Message text is not stored in the progress database.
  • Operational configuration: server and role identifiers, role-color snapshots, and feature state may be stored locally so configured actions can be restored or repeated safely.

How the information is used

Information is used only to operate Geekbot: responding to commands, enforcing server rules, managing roles and onboarding, validating posts or event submissions, generating requested media, diagnosing failures, and protecting the bot and community from abuse.

Storage and retention

Persistent Geekbot data is kept in local databases on systems controlled by the operator. Newcomer progress is deleted after graduation and pruned when it is no longer needed, subject to short retention for rejoin handling. Operational role snapshots and state are retained while needed for their feature. Temporary downloaded or generated media is discarded after processing.

Sharing

Geekbot does not sell personal data, serve advertising, or use member data to train machine-learning or AI models. Data is not shared with third parties for their own marketing. Discord necessarily processes information as the platform hosting the bot; content fetched from a user-supplied media link may also be handled by that link's hosting provider.

Direct messages and public access

Geekbot's configured features operate in authorized servers and are not designed to read or archive direct messages. The bot is not offered for arbitrary public installation.

Your choices

You may ask the staff of the Discord server where Geekbot is operating to review or delete data associated with your Discord account. A request may be limited where data is still needed for security, active moderation, legal obligations, or prevention of ongoing abuse.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when Geekbot's features or data practices change. The current version will remain available at this URL. For questions or requests, contact the staff of the Discord server where Geekbot is operating.
